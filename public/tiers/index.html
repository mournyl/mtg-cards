how it's ranked

One scale for every list. Each card gets a 0–100 score for each job it does, judged for a four-player Bracket 3 table: efficiency (30 points: mana value for the effect, free or alternate costs), reach (25: what it can hit or how much it does), speed (15: instant or flash), value (15: card advantage, a body left behind, more than one thing handled) and reliability (15: no symmetry, no tax they can pay, no condition, no gift to opponents). An A in ramp means the same as an A in removal.

Tiers by score: S 90+, A 75–89, B 60–74, C 45–59, D 30–44, F under 30. Picking colors keeps the same scores and adds a rank among the cards shown, so white's best card shows first without an inflated tier.

Hand-reviewed vs unreviewed. Every list's top cards were placed by hand, with a one-line reason that names the cost and the catch. The rest are scored by the detectors (the card details say so); their scores are squeezed into C, D and F (58 at most) until someone reviews them. Anchor cards keep lists consistent: Counterspell, Swords to Plowshares, Cultivate, Phyrexian Arena and Wrath of God.

Tags never change a score: Game Changer, free to cast, and the EDHREC play rate where we have it. Sub-lists rank cards only against cards that do the same job, and a card that does two jobs sits on both lists.

Colors follow color identity: mana cost, color indicator, mana symbols in the rules text and basic land types. Pick one color or several: a card shows when all of its colors are picked, and colorless cards show when Colorless is picked, so picking your commander's colors plus Colorless shows everything that fits the deck.

Pool: every card legal in Commander with a paper printing, from the Forge card database, rebuilt every week.
