how it's ranked

One scale for every list. Each card gets a 0–100 score for each job it does, judged for a four-player Bracket 3 table: efficiency (30 points: mana value for the effect, free or alternate costs), reach (25: what it can hit or how much it does), speed (15: instant or flash), value (15: card advantage, a body left behind, more than one thing handled) and reliability (15: no symmetry, no tax they can pay, no condition, no gift to opponents). An A in ramp means the same as an A in removal.

Tiers by score: S 90+, A 75–89, B 60–74, C 45–59, D 30–44, F under 30. Color pages sort by the same score and add a best-in-color rank, so white's best card shows first without an inflated tier.

Hand-reviewed vs unreviewed. Every list's top cards were placed by hand, with a one-line reason that names the cost and the catch. The rest are scored by the detectors and carry an Unreviewed badge; their scores are squeezed into C, D and F (58 at most) until someone reviews them. Anchor cards keep lists consistent: Counterspell, Swords to Plowshares, Cultivate, Phyrexian Arena and Wrath of God.

Badges never change a score: Game Changer, free to cast, new this year, and the EDHREC play rate where we have it. Sub-lists rank cards only against cards that do the same job, and a card that does two jobs sits on both lists.

Colors follow color identity: mana cost, color indicator, mana symbols in the rules text and basic land types. Single-color pages hold mono-color cards; Multicolor holds the rest; My colors merges every card that fits your commander.

Pool: every card legal in Commander with a paper printing, from the Forge card database, rebuilt every week.
